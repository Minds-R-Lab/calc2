Chapter 1

Integration by Parts: Master the Technique

Integration by parts is your first advanced technique—it undoes the product rule to help you integrate products of functions. In this video, you'll see the formula $\int u \, dv = uv - \int v \, du$ in action. We'll work through classic examples like $\int x e^x \, dx$ and $\int x \sin(x) \, dx$, showing how to choose $u$ and $dv$ strategically (using the ILATE rule). By the end, you'll tackle integrals that seemed impossible with basic methods.

📗 Integration Techniques ⏱️ Duration: 22 min
Chapter 2

Volumes of Revolution: Disk, Washer, and Shell Methods

When you rotate a 2D region around an axis, you create a 3D solid. But how do you find its volume? In this video, we explore three powerful methods: the disk method (slicing perpendicular to the axis), the washer method (for solids with holes), and the shell method (slicing parallel to the axis). Each method uses integration in a different way. We'll solve problems like finding the volume of a cone, a torus, and more complex shapes. You'll see how the choice of method matters.
